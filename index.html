Research Assistant · SLMC Lab, University of Edinburgh

I try to make robots learn how to use their body like a baby. I work on robot learning, dexterous manipulation, and reinforcement learning. I did my MSc in AI at the University of Edinburgh, where I worked on blind in-hand dexterous manipulation using tactile sensing, and my BTech in Information Technology at NITK Surathkal (2025). I'm currently a Research Assistant at the SLMC lab, working on fault anomalies in contact-rich manipulation.
