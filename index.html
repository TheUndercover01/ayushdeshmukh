About meBio

I'm a Research Assistant at the SLMC lab at the University of Edinburgh, working with Prof. Sethu Vijayakumar. These days I study what happens when things go wrong in contact-rich manipulation, because robots, like babies, learn a lot from knocking things over. More broadly, I'm into robot learning, dexterous manipulation, and reinforcement learning.

I did my MSc in AI at Edinburgh, where I taught a robot hand to manipulate objects blind, using only its sense of touch. Before that, I did my BTech in IT at NITK Surathkal (2025) with Prof. Anand Kumar, and spent time at the CANDLE lab at IIT Roorkee with Prof. Sparsh Mittal.

When I'm not bothering robots, you'll find me sleeping, walking, eating, or rewatching Suits for the hundredth time, always with a coffee in hand (probably one too many). That's pretty much me, in a nutshell.
